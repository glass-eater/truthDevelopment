In a country, the government has his own laws and policies. Likewise, in a family, there is also an ethical code of conduct to follow. Unexcep. tionally, truth has its own set of rules and regulations too. If one abides and follows it accordingly, one is able to achieve perfection in the long run, follows it accordingly, one is able to achieve perfection in the long run. 

We should always follow the rules and regulations set by our God. Those who are first informed should instruct those who come later, and those who first apprehend the Code should instruct those who are slower to grasp it. This is one of man's responsibilities. It is the way of Heaven to be altruistic and collaborate with others in the performance of good. When those who first apprehend the Code instruct those who are slower in learning it, they practice the Code of Heaven and accelerate the progress of mankind 

We must aim at the highest goal: be far-sighted; strict mannerism; setting a good example for others to follow; be merciful; good personality; just and patient; respectable to elders; and be humble all the time. Do not speak evil words; do not hurt the feelings of others. We must endure all hardships and sacrifices. Avoid worldly entanglements, adopt vegetarian diets, advocate loving and helping others, practice self-denial, exhort good conduct, inhibit killing, curb avarice and counsel a healthy attitude. We must distinguish between the demons and be acknowledgeable to truth. Be patient during the hardtimes of truth development. Only in this way can good. ness be continued, nature fulfilled, virtue achieved and the Code nurtured,
