Sky and earth are changeable and as such there will be a beginning and an end. The life-cycle of the sky and the earth from the time it is created by our God to the time of its doomsday will take a total of 129,600 years. It is sub-divided into twelve periods, each period consist of 10,800 years. The sky is created during the first period, earth the second and man the third. After the seventh period, everything will be subjecting to gradual destruction and so it is the turning point. Man will be destroyed in the ninth period whereas in the tenth period the whole earth will be under complete destruction. The eleventh period saw the sky being des- troyed and in the twelfth period, both the sky and the earth will be totally disappeared.

During the 'green stage' there were only nine disasters, the numbers is small because during that time, mankind were most humane and decent. At that time, truth was only given to kings and ministers of the state. Out of the ninety-six billions of original souls, only two billions were saved. The God appointed by our God to take charge of Heaven during this stage was 'Lian Teng' Saint and in the affairs of truth, 'Hock Hee' Saint. The 'red stage' save another two out of the ninety-four billions being saved by Shakyamoni Saint who was in charge of Heaven and Nee Toe Saint who was in charge of truth. 

Now, the 'white stage' was controlled by Holy Spirit Saint (laughing Saint) who is in charge of Heaven and Chae Kong Saint who is in charge of spreading truth. This last stage shall see a total of eighty-one number of disasters, the worst amongst all the three periods because by now, man's heart have deteriorated.
